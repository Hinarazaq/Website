About me

Psychology, education,
and genuine care

I'm Hina Razaq, a clinical psychologist based in Islamabad with experience spanning mental health, child development, special needs education and psychosocial support.

My career has taken me from inpatient psychiatric wards at PIMS to rehabilitation centres and special schools, giving me a broad, grounded perspective on what people actually need to thrive.

I am a clinical psychologist with a Masters Degree in Applied Psychology and an Advanced Diploma in Clinical Psychology. I am also certified in the Griffiths III Child Developmental Assessment. I offer online sessions that are warm, evidence-informed and adapted to you.

Questions you might have

How long is a session and how much does it cost?+

Each session lasts 40 minutes and costs PKR 3,000.

Where do sessions happen?+

All sessions are online, over Zoom or Google Meet, so you can join from wherever you feel comfortable. Scheduling is flexible across time zones.

Is what I share kept confidential?+

Yes. Sessions are confidential and non-judgemental: a safe space to talk openly.

Do you work with children?+

Yes. I offer child psychology support and developmental assessments (I'm certified in Griffiths III), and I work with parents and teachers too.

What happens after I send a request?+

I'll be in touch within 24–48 hours to confirm your slot and share session details. The first conversation is about getting to know each other, with no pressure and no commitment.
