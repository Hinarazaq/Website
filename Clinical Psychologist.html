About me

Psychology, education,
and genuine care

I'm Hina Razaq, a clinical psychologist based in Islamabad with experience spanning mental health, child development, special needs education and psychosocial support.

My career has taken me from inpatient psychiatric wards at PIMS to rehabilitation centres and special schools, giving me a broad, grounded perspective on what people actually need to thrive.

I currently hold an Advanced Diploma in Clinical Psychology from NUML University and am certified in the Griffiths III Child Developmental Assessment. I offer online sessions that are warm, evidence-informed, and adapted to you.

My approach

How a session
works

1
Initial consultation
We talk about what brings you here, what you're hoping for, and whether we're a good fit. No pressure, no commitment.
2
Assessment & formulation
I take time to understand your background, your strengths, and your goals and we build a shared picture together.
3
Ongoing sessions
Regular online sessions, flexible to your schedule. We work at your pace, checking in on progress as we go.
4
Review & next steps
We periodically review what's working and adapt the approach because your needs will shift, and so will our work.
